About the workshop

A France–Hong Kong multidisciplinary collaboration

This workshop brings together experts in oncology, surgery, radiology, pathology and data science to advance precision care in ovarian cancer. Through scientific presentations, interactive case discussions and live demonstrations, it explores surgical innovation, HIPEC, molecular stratification, treatment response assessment and AI integration.

The meeting also provides a forum to strengthen France–Hong Kong collaborations, develop joint research projects and identify opportunities for future clinical trials.
